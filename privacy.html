TheFenix - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

TheFenix attaches great importance to protecting your privacy. Before using the services or applications provided by TheFenix, please read this Privacy Policy carefully.

In order to provide more accurate and well-targeted services, TheFenix may use personal information submitted or generated by you in the manners described below. TheFenix will treat such information with a high level of care and responsibility.

Except as described in this Privacy Policy, TheFenix will not disclose your personal information or provide such information to third parties without your permission, unless required by applicable law.

This Privacy Policy describes what personal information you may share with applications provided by TheFenix, how such information is received, collected, processed and used, and the choices available to you regarding the use of your information.

Information Collected and Received

When you install or use TheFenix applications, TheFenix may collect, process and retain information from you and from devices that you use to interact with our services.

Depending on the application and the features you use, this information may include:

  • Geo-location information;
  • IP address;
  • Device ID or other unique identifiers;
  • Device manufacturer and device type;
  • Device and hardware settings;
  • Advertising identifiers;
  • Advertising-related data;
  • Operating system information;
  • Network operator information;
  • IMSI, where applicable and permitted;
  • Network and connection information;
  • Screen resolution;
  • Application usage statistics;
  • Device logs and event information;
  • Application version information;
  • Information generated through your interaction with our applications and services.

Certain applications may request access to call, message or similar device information when such access is necessary for the application's functionality and is permitted by the operating system and applicable laws.

Where applicable, this information may include information relating to incoming and outgoing calls or messages, such as timestamps or call duration.

How We Use Information

TheFenix may use information collected through our applications and services for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating application features;
  • Maintaining and improving our applications and services;
  • Developing new features and services;
  • Diagnosing errors, crashes and technical problems;
  • Improving application performance and user experience;
  • Protecting TheFenix, our applications and our users;
  • Providing relevant or personalized content where applicable;
  • Providing advertising that may be more relevant to users, where permitted.

Device Permissions

Some TheFenix applications may request specific device permissions when those permissions are required to provide a particular feature.

For example, some applications may request Device Administrator permission in order to provide screen-locking functionality.

Permissions are requested only where required by an application's functionality. You may review or revoke certain permissions through your device settings. Revoking a required permission may cause some application features to stop functioning correctly.

Third-Party Services

Some TheFenix applications may use third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers or advertising services.

These third parties may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. The information collected may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, usage information and other technical information.

Data Security

TheFenix takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure or alteration.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and therefore absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and operating system, you may be able to control certain data collection through your device settings, including application permissions, location access and advertising preferences.

You may also stop further collection of information by uninstalling the relevant application from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

TheFenix may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our applications, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

Any changes will be published on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
